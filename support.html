SpellPack Support

SpellPack is an iPad spelling-practice app for young children, built around Apple Pencil and the Look · Cover · Write · Check method. Need help? Email info@cognitech.dev and we'll get back to you.

Frequently asked

Does SpellPack need the internet?
No. Everything runs on the device. No account or sign-in is needed.
Is my child's data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere; all work stays on your iPad.
How do I get a clearer reading voice?
In iPad Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Spoken Content ▸ Voices, download an Enhanced or Premium English voice, then pick it in SpellPack's grown-up Settings.
How do I reset all the data?
Open SpellPack's Settings ▸ Erase all data, enter the grown-ups password, then reopen the app for a fresh start.
