SpellPack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

The short version: We do not collect your app data. There are no accounts, ads, tracking, or third-party analytics. SpellPack stores spelling lists, learning progress, handwriting, and optional parent voice recordings locally on your iPad. Camera scanning, handwriting recognition, and pronunciation checks run on the device. SpellPack does not upload this content.

Who this is for

SpellPack is a spelling-practice app designed for children (ages 6–8) and the grown-ups who help them. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information. It applies to the SpellPack iPad app published by the developer (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect

We do not receive data from the app. SpellPack does not upload your child's learning data, spelling lists, handwriting, photos, or parent voice recordings to us or third-party services. Specifically:
  • No account, sign-in, name, email, or contact details are required or requested by us. (You may type a child's first name to personalise the app — this is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.)
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers are accessed for tracking.

Where your child's work is stored

Practice sessions, spelling results, stars, saved handwriting, school spelling lists, and the in-app companion (“Buddy”) are stored locally on your iPad using Apple's on-device database. Optional parent voice recordings are saved as audio files in the app's local storage. SpellPack does not upload this content. If you use Apple's standard device backup (iCloud or your computer), that backup is controlled by you and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Microphone & speech

SpellPack can listen when your child reads a word aloud, to check their pronunciation. This uses Apple's on-device speech recognition only — the audio is analysed on your iPad and is not saved or sent to any server (ours or Apple's). If a device cannot perform on-device recognition, the listening feature is simply skipped rather than sending audio anywhere.

Separately, the optional parent voice studio lets a grown-up intentionally record spelling words for dictation. These recordings are saved locally on the iPad and played back during practice. SpellPack does not upload them. Deleting a school list deletes its associated saved recordings. The microphone is used only during an active pronunciation check or a recording started by the user.

Camera and selected photos

You can optionally scan a printed school spelling list with the camera or choose an image from your photo library. Apple's on-device Vision technology extracts candidate words for you to review before saving the list. SpellPack does not save a copy of the source image or upload it. Your original photo remains subject to your own photo-library and backup settings.

Handwriting

Handwriting written with Apple Pencil or a finger is recognised on the device using Apple's on-device Vision technology, to check spelling. Handwriting stays on the device and is not transmitted.

On-device generative features

On supported systems, some spelling hints and Buddy stories use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Text is processed locally. SpellPack does not send these prompts to a remote AI service. Curated alternatives are available when the on-device model is unavailable.

Children's privacy

SpellPack is designed for children. We do not receive, sell, or share their learning content. There are no behavioural ads or third-party tracking SDKs. Links that lead outside the app (such as this page) are placed behind a parental gate.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page.
