SpellPack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

The short version: SpellPack collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. Everything your child does stays on your iPad. Your child's voice and handwriting are processed entirely on the device and are never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Who this is for

SpellPack is a spelling-practice app designed for children (ages 6–8) and the grown-ups who help them. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information. It applies to the SpellPack iPad app published by the developer (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect

None. SpellPack does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information from anyone, including children. Specifically:
  • No account, sign-in, name, email, or contact details are required or requested by us. (You may type a child's first name to personalise the app — this is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.)
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers are accessed for tracking.

Where your child's work is stored

All learning data — practice sessions, spelling results, stars, handwriting, and the in-app companion (“Buddy”) — is stored locally on your iPad using Apple's on-device database. It never leaves the device through SpellPack. If you use Apple's standard device backup (iCloud or your computer), that backup is controlled by you and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Microphone & speech

SpellPack can listen when your child reads a word aloud, to check their pronunciation. This uses Apple's on-device speech recognition only — the audio is analysed on your iPad and is never recorded, saved, or sent to any server (ours or Apple's). If a device cannot perform on-device recognition, the listening feature is simply skipped rather than sending audio anywhere. The microphone is used only while a listening activity is active.

Handwriting

Handwriting written with Apple Pencil or a finger is recognised on the device using Apple's on-device Vision technology, to check spelling. Handwriting stays on the device and is not transmitted.

Children's privacy

SpellPack is designed to be safe for children and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR provisions for children. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to misuse, sell, or disclose. There are no behavioural ads and no third-party data sharing. Links that lead outside the app (such as this page) are placed behind a parental gate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Since the app collects no data, any changes will be minor.
